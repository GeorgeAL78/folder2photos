Sign in with Google

  1. Enter the Client ID and Client secret of your Google Cloud "Desktop app" client (Photos Library API enabled).
  2. Click Sign in with Google. A Google tab opens. Choose your account and allow access.
  3. Google then sends you to a page that fails to load (address starts with http://127.0.0.1:53682/). That is expected. Copy the whole address from the address bar.
  4. Paste it below and click Finish.
